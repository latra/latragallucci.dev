# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience is recruiters, hiring managers and research leads with limited time, deciding whether to reach out. Secondary audience is peers, collaborators and students checking credibility or looking for a specific tool (e.g. the Xarxes lab game). Context is a quick scan on a laptop between meetings, not a deep read.

## Product Purpose

The personal site of Paula Gallucci Zurita — software engineer, research software engineer at Eurecat and computer networks instructor at the Universitat de Lleida. It presents who she is, what she works on now, a curated index of projects with honest status, and her experience. Success is a visitor understanding her focus (backend and networked systems, teaching) in under 30 seconds and emailing her.

## Positioning

Serious and professional. The work carries the page; the presentation stays out of its way. No gamification: no XP, levels, achievements, locked content or game vocabulary ("quests", "missions").

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: email, surfaced in the contact band on the home page and in the footer of every page.
- Proof: current roles, project write-ups with stack, period, status and source links, and the experience list.

## Brand Personality

Precise, unpretentious, instructional — like a well-made lab handout. Confident without self-promotion.

## Anti-references

- The previous gamified version of this site (XP bars, trophy tiers, cursor dot, neon on near-black).
- AI-template tells: eyebrows above every heading, stat banner rows, rounded glowing cards, condensed caps with tracking, fade-up-on-scroll for every block.

## Design Principles

Lead with what she does now. Keep every project honest about its status (completed, in progress, concept, discontinued). Contact is never more than one click away. The site's own execution — speed, accessibility, typography — is part of the proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in both light and dark modes; keyboard focus visible; skip link; reduced motion respected.
