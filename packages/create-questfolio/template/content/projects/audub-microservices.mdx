**AU-DUB** is an attempt at a fully automated dubbing pipeline: drop in a video, and a chain of independent services should transcribe it, translate the transcript, clone the original speaker's voice, synthesize the translated audio, and mount it back onto the video — no manual editing in between.

The architecture is split into decoupled services talking over **RabbitMQ**, with **MongoDB** holding job state and metadata between stages:

- **Preprocessing** — pulls audio out of the source video and prepares it for the pipeline.
- **Transcription** — speech-to-text via **OpenAI Whisper**.
- **Translation** — moves the transcript between languages using a **Llama** model.
- **Text-to-Speech** — synthesizes and clones the voice with **Coqui XTTS**.
- **Mounter** — stitches the dubbed audio back onto the original video.

Everything is containerized with **Docker Compose**, each service with its own Python requirements and configuration, so in theory the whole queue can scale horizontally as jobs pile up.

In practice, this is the project that refuses to be finished. Every service works in isolation, the queue design is solid on paper, and there's always *one more* model, codec edge case, or sync-drift bug between audio and video that reopens the whole thing. It's less a shipped tool at this point and more a standing invitation to come back and chase the dubbing pipeline a little further down the rabbit hole.
