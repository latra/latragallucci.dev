**Blood Bowl Manager Bot** brings tabletop league management into Discord for **Blood Bowl**, the Warhammer Fantasy take on American football. Instead of jumping to a website to check who's playing whom this round, a league's community can query everything without leaving the server.

Configuring a tournament with `bb2!config` binds the bot to a specific league and tournament name from **GoblinSpy** (mordrek.com's Blood Bowl league data source), after which `bb2!teams` lists the competing rosters and `bb2!round` shows the current or a specified round's matchups. `bb2!reset` clears the configuration, and `bb2!help` lists everything above for anyone who wanders in mid-season.

Built with **Python**, **discord.py** and a local **SQLite** database to hold league state between commands, it was a small collaborative build with Mikel Ocejo, standing on top of Andreas Harrison's GoblinSpy data.
