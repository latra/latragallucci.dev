**Ludens Servers** starts from a familiar problem: a group of friends wants to play a game together on a dedicated server, but running one means someone has to become the reluctant sysadmin — SSH-ing in, editing config files, restarting containers, and being the only person who knows how any of it works.

The idea is to collapse all of that into something a non-technical group can drive themselves: pick a game, spin up its dedicated server, and switch to a different game or a different world/save with a couple of clicks — no terminal, no manifest files, no "ask the one friend who knows Linux."

Still at the idea stage — the concrete stack isn't locked in yet, but the shape of the problem is: an orchestration layer that manages containerized game servers behind a simple control surface built for people who just want to play, not administer.
